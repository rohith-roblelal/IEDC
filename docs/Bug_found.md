## IEDC Website – Bug Testing Report
## Testing Status: Bugs Identified During Website Testing
## Date: 08 August 2026
## 1. Collaborative Partner Logos
### Issue:
Collaborative partner logos on the homepage appear too small.
The white background around the logos could be removed for a cleaner appearance.
Clicking on a partner logo does not display the partner's details.
## Expected:
Increase the size of the logos.
Use transparent backgrounds where possible.
Clicking a logo should open/display the respective partner's details.

## 2. Unable to Delete Co-Founder
## Section: Startup
### Issue:
The co-founder cannot be deleted from the Startup section.
Delete button is not working.
Cancel button is not working.
Close (X) button is also not working.

## 3. Events Not Appearing on Homepage
## Section: Events
### Issue:
Even after successfully adding an event through the admin panel, the event does not appear on the homepage.

## 4. Event Posters Not Displaying
## Section: Events
### Issue:
Event posters uploaded/added through the admin panel are not being displayed.

## 5. Unable to Delete Student Lead
## Section: Student Leads
### Issue:
When attempting to remove a student lead, the deletion fails and displays the message:
"Failed to delete"

## 6. Unable to Delete Collaborative Partner
## Section: Collaborative Partners
### Issue:
Attempting to delete a partner results in a "Failed to delete" error.

## 7. Unable to Delete Announcements
## Section: Announcements
### Issue:
Announcements cannot be deleted from the admin panel.

## 8. Deleted Posters Still Appearing on Homepage
## Section: Posters
### Issue:
Posters that are deleted from the admin panel continue to appear on the homepage.

## 9. Images Added in Settings Not Displaying
## Section: Settings
### Issue:
Images added through the Settings section are not appearing on the website.
